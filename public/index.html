    Common questions

    Before you get in touch.

    Short answers to the questions people ask most often before a first enquiry.

    Before you write

    What should I prepare for a first consultation?

    A short timeline of what happened, the names of the people or companies involved, and copies of any notice, agreement or order you have received. Keep them ready, and share them only once the firm confirms a secure way to send them.

    Your enquiry

    Does sending an enquiry make you my lawyer?

    No. A lawyer-client relationship begins only when the firm agrees in writing to act for you. Until then, please share only a brief, non-confidential overview.

    Next steps

    What happens after I send an enquiry?

    The firm reviews it, checks whether it can assist and whether there is any conflict of interest, then explains the scope of work and applicable fees before anything begins.

    Start a conversation

    The right next step starts with clarity.

    Tell us briefly what you need help with. The firm can then explain whether it may be able to assist and what happens next.
